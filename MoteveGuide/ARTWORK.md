# Skin artwork

## 0.6.0 navigation assets

The six `nav_*.tga` assets are original, deterministic polygon/arc geometry generated by `build_nav_assets.py`, provided in the 0.6.0 source package. They are 128×128 uncompressed RGBA TGA images. Pointer assets point north so the existing direction calculation remains unchanged. The compass and glass disc are separate, nonrotating layers. The approved ImageGen comparison sheets were visual references only; they were not cropped into the shipped textures. Earlier artwork and font credits below remain applicable.

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Atlas regions were exported as uncompressed RGBA TGA assets, with aspect-preserving quest symbols. Existing fonts and original utility textures retain their supplied credits.
